## 🚀 What AppDowngrader Does

AppDowngrader helps you roll an iOS app back to an older version. This can help if a new update removed a feature, changed the layout, or caused problems on your device.

Use it to:

- install an older app build
- keep a version you know works
- test app changes across versions
- avoid issues from a recent update

## 💻 What You Need

Before you start, make sure you have:

- a Windows PC
- a stable internet connection
- an iPhone or iPad
- the app file or backup you want to downgrade
- enough free space on your PC and device

For the best results, keep your iPhone or iPad connected with a good cable during the process.

## 🔌 Connect Your iPhone or iPad

Before you downgrade an app, connect your device:

1. Use a USB cable to connect your iPhone or iPad to your PC.

## 🧩 Troubleshooting

If something goes wrong, try these steps:

- close AppDowngrader and open it again
- unplug the device and connect it again
- use another USB port
- use another cable
- unlock your iPhone or iPad
- tap **Trust This Computer** if asked
- restart your PC
- restart your iPhone or iPad

If the app still does not detect your device, check that your Apple drivers are installed on Windows.
